Study Overview

The training dataset follows 10 randomized subjects across Baseline, Week 4, Week 8 and Week 12, with demographics, vital signs, laboratory data and adverse events.

📋

Study Design

Simulated Phase III hypertension study with CardioPress and Placebo treatment arms.

🧾

Data Collection

Subject-level demographics plus repeated vital signs and laboratory assessments.

⚠️

Safety Data

Five adverse-event records covering term, severity, relatedness and outcome.
