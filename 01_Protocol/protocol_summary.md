# Protocol Summary — HTN-CDM-001

**Study:** Phase III randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study of fictional CardioPress 10 mg in adults with mild-to-moderate hypertension.

- Treatment: CardioPress 10 mg vs Placebo
- Treatment duration: 12 weeks
- Population: Adults 18–65 years
- Primary endpoint: Change in seated systolic blood pressure (SBP) from baseline to Week 12
- Safety: Adverse events, vital signs and laboratory parameters
- Data status: Fully simulated educational data; no real patient data.
